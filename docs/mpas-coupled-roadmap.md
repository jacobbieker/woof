# MPAS ocean, sea-ice and land-ice: port roadmap

woof's hex lane is a port of MPAS-Atmosphere v8.4.2. The same MPAS-Model
snapshot (MPAS-Dev/MPAS-Model v8.4.2, `f34984b2c89353cf31c235b08d3b4acd96b37bb3`)
carries three more cores: `core_ocean` (MPAS-Ocean), `core_seaice`
(MPAS-Seaice) and `core_landice` (MPAS land-ice, MALI). This page records
what is ported from those three cores, how each piece is held to a reference,
and what remains.

## What existed before these cores

These are separate models. None of them is a port of the three cores, and the
cores do not replace them:

| Module | What it is |
|---|---|
| `woof/hex/mpasphys/seaice_v842.py` | MPAS-Atmosphere's sea-ice *surface* treatment (the Noah sea-ice module MPAS-A calls). It is atmospheric physics over a prescribed ice cover. |
| `woof/hex/mpasphys/oml_v842.py`, `woof/hex/ocean_mixed_layer.py` | MPAS-A's slab ocean mixed layer (WRF `module_sf_oml.F`). |
| `woof/ocean/seaice` | An original zero-layer thermodynamic sea-ice model (Semtner 1976) for the WRF lane. It contains no Icepack or MPAS-Seaice source. |
| `woof/ocean/mixed_layer` | WRF 4.6.1's 3-D PWP ocean and the Pollard mixed layer. |
| `woof/ocean/waves`, `woof/ocean/surge` | The wave and storm-surge models. |

## Doors and the coupling spine

`woof hex seaice|ocean|ice {status,options,verify}` reports each core's port
status, the pinned oracle and the strict recipe. It also lists every option
the core's `Registry.xml` declares (276 sea-ice, 248 ocean, 72 land-ice
options). The inventories are generated by `tools/mpas_coupled_options.py` and
pinned by the Registry's sha256.

`woof hex coupled` plans one wall clock for the coupled cores:

- the cadence must divide every component's timestep;
- the run length must be a multiple of the cadence;
- a flux channel must be conserved to the bit;
- a core that is not yet ported is refused by name.

A core is `ported` only when its dynamical core and every scheme run on the
GPU and its landing gate passes. Until then its status is `stub`, or
`partial` once its standalone forward mode runs word for word on the GPU;
a coupled step refuses a `partial` core as it refuses a stub. A core may be
`ported_subset`: a coupled step runs it with the schemes it declares and
refuses any other by name (MALI: the 'sia', 'simple' and 'none' velocity solvers).

## The reference

MPAS v8.4.2 builds in single precision by default (`Makefile`:
"single is the default"). The hex port pins that build. The new cores use the
same strict recipe:

- GNU Fortran, `-O0 -ffp-contract=off`;
- `RKIND` = 4 for the core's own `real(kind=RKIND)` arithmetic.

The Icepack-lineage column package (`core_seaice/column`, `ice_kinds_mod`'s
`dbl_kind`) is double precision whatever `RKIND` is, so a column-physics port
is double. On Metal that means woof's soft-f64. MPAS-Ocean is run in double
precision in practice, so its pieces are held at both RKIND 4 and RKIND 8.

Each piece is a *targeted extraction*. The routines are compiled verbatim and
checked against a `SOURCES.sha256`. The only stand-ins are the framework types
the routines walk (pools, blocks, timers), and those carry no arithmetic. A
driver feeds known inputs and writes the outputs as raw words. The
`mpas-oracle` pixi environment (`pixi run -e mpas-oracle ...`, linux-64)
supplies gfortran. Tests that need the oracle build skip by name when it is
absent, and committed fixtures under `tests/data/oracles/` keep the word
comparison running without it.

## MPAS-Seaice (`core_seaice`, about 99,000 lines)

Ordered by how self-contained each piece is:

1. **EVP internal stress, variational discretisation** (`shared/mpas_seaice_velocity_solver_variational.F`,
   `..._constitutive_relation.F`): strain tensor, EVP and revised-EVP
   constitutive relation, stress divergence, and `seaice_init_evp`. These are
   the inner loop of the elastic subcycle.
2. The rest of the elastic subcycle (`mpas_seaice_velocity_solver.F`). Ported:
   the Coriolis coefficient, ocean stress, subcycle initialisation, ocean
   stress coefficient, `solve_velocity` and `solve_velocity_revised`, and the
   principal stresses, composed with item 1 into whole subcycles; and the
   other pre-subcycle routines (mass and area aggregation, masks,
   `new_ice_velocities`, the Hibler `ice_strength`, `air_stress`,
   `surface_tilt`, with `seaice_mesh`'s cell-to-vertex interpolation) and
   `ocean_stress_final` (with its vertex-to-cell interpolation), so the whole
   of `seaice_run_velocity_solver` for the variational scheme, with the
   column package's ice strength (`colpkg_ice_strength`, Rothrock75 and
   Hibler79; `column_ridge_v842.ice_strength`). The column package is
   double, so production MPAS-Seaice is built double: each seaice piece is
   held at RKIND 4 and 8.
3. The basis-function setup for the variational solver (Wachspress and PWL,
   `..._wachspress.F`, `..._pwl.F`). This is init-time work and can stay on the host.
   Its outputs (`basisGradientU/V`, `basisIntegrals*`) are the inputs the
   kernels in item 1 read.
4. The weak-form solver (`..._weak.F`), an alternative velocity discretisation.
5. Incremental-remap advection (`mpas_seaice_advection_incremental_remap*.F`)
   on planar and spherical meshes. Ported: the tracer tree the packages build, the
   volume/thickness conversions, the departure points and triangles, the masks
   and limited linear reconstruction, the flux integration, the mass and tracer
   update, the mean-recovery check and the zap of tiny areas, and the remap
   inside the forward time step (`config_use_advection`, each dynamics
   subcycle, N steps with a bitwise restart). On a sphere: the gradient through
   the rotated grid (`config_rotate_cartesian_grid`) and the cell's local frame,
   and the departure triangles' edge-vector basis in
   `shift_vertices_of_departure_triangle`, on hexagons and quadrilaterals about
   either pole and at mid-latitude, uniform and stretched. The init's geometry
   (`seaice_init_advection_incremental_remap`: the rotated grid, the local
   frames, the vertices in each plane, the remap neighbours, the moments, and
   MPAS's RBF set-up with `coeffs_reconstruct`), computed on the host from the
   mesh at init (`woof/hex/mpas_seaice/remap_init_v842.py`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_remap_init_parity.py`); periodic planar meshes are
   refused by name. The optional
   checks (`config_conservation_check`: the sums before and after the update
   and `check_tracer_conservation`; `config_monotonicity_check`: the local
   extremes, their in-place extension and the test), one rank, with v8.4.2's
   error messages and codes (`tests/test_mpas_seaice_remap_checks_parity.py`).
   Refused by name: halo exchanges. Upwind advection
   cannot run in v8.4.2 (it reads configs the sea-ice Registry does not declare
   and mesh fields it does not carry), so it is refused by name with that
   reason.
6. Column physics (`column/`, colpkg), double precision only
   (`mpas_seaice_column.F` does not compile at RKIND 4). Ported: the
   initialisation and aggregation (`woof/hex/mpas_seaice/column_v842.py`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_column_parity.py`): the tracer object,
   `colpkg_init_itd`, `colpkg_init_thermo` (graded for 1 to 64 ice layers),
   `colpkg_init_trcr`, the `cice_default`, `uniform` and `no_ice` initial
   states, `initialize_coupler_fields`, `seaice_reset_coupler_fluxes`,
   `colpkg_aggregate` and `seaice_column_update_state`. Also ported:
   `seaice_column_vertical_thermodynamics` with BL99 (bubbly or MU71
   conductivity, CCSM3 atmosphere boundary layer, coupled or computed surface
   temperature; `woof/hex/mpas_seaice/column_therm_v842.py`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_column_therm_parity.py`). It runs only behind
   `WOOF_GLIBC239_LIBM64=1`: `psimhu`/`psixhu` call atan, glibc's atan is
   not correctly rounded, and the port matches it word for word only through
   the LGPL transcription. Also ported: `column_itd_thermodynamics`
   (`colpkg_step_therm2`: `linear_itd` or the delta function,
   `add_new_ice` for BL99 and mushy, `lateral_melt`, `reduce_area` and
   `cleanup_itd`, with level, CESM or topo ponds;
   `woof/hex/mpas_seaice/column_itd_v842.py`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_column_itd_parity.py`), which calls no libm and
   needs no opt-in. Also ported: `column_ridging` (`colpkg_step_ridge`:
   `ridge_ice` with the Thorndike75 or exponential participation and the
   Hibler80 or exponential redistribution, then `cleanup_itd`;
   `woof/hex/mpas_seaice/column_ridge_v842.py`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_column_ridge_parity.py`). Its `exp` is glibc's,
   transcribed under MIT, so it needs no opt-in. Where v8.4.2 itself dies
   (`ridge_shift`'s aborts hit a gfortran runtime error writing their label),
   the port raises the same error. Also ported: `column_radiation` without
   delta-Eddington (`colpkg_step_radiation`: `shortwave_ccsm3` with
   `constant_albedos` or `compute_albedos` and `absorbed_solar`, the coupled
   shortwave), `column_prep_radiation` and `seaice_init_column_shortwave`
   (`woof/hex/mpas_seaice/column_radiation_v842.py`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_column_radiation_parity.py`). `absorbed_solar`'s
   exp is the MIT transcription; `compute_albedos`' atan needs the LGPL
   opt-in. v8.4.2 passes `ncat` as `absorbed_solar`'s layer count; the port
   keeps that and refuses `ncat > nilyr`, where v8.4.2 writes past the array.
   Also ported: the delta-Eddington shortwave with a computed surface
   temperature (`colpkg_init_orbit`, `compute_coszen`, `run_dEdd` with
   level, CESM, topo or no ponds; `column_dedd_v842.cu`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_column_dedd_parity.py`). Its orbit and solar
   zenith angle call glibc's sin, cos, atan and asin, so it needs the LGPL
   opt-in; aerosols and the bioabsorption stay refused.
   Also ported: the coupling preparation, `seaice_column_coupling_prep` with
   `seaice_column_scale_fluxes` (the saved freezing/melting potential, the
   cell albedos and effective pond area, the pond freshwater feedback, the
   per-area copies, the shortwave scaling factor, then the fluxes per unit
   ice area or the open-water values;
   `woof/hex/mpas_seaice/coupling_prep_v842.py`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_coupling_prep_parity.py`). Cut out of
   `mpas_seaice_column.F`, the two routines compile at RKIND 4 too, so this
   piece is word-exact at both precisions. It calls no libm. The stand-alone
   ocean mixed layer it runs first (`config_use_ocean_mixed_layer`, `.true.`
   in the Registry, off when coupled to an ocean:
   `seaice_column_ocean_mixed_layer`, the open ocean's `atmo_boundary_layer`
   and `colpkg_ocn_mixed_layer`) is ported at RKIND 8, word-exact alone and
   inside the column step (`woof/hex/mpas_seaice/ocean_mixed_layer_v842.py`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_ocean_mixed_layer_parity.py`; glibc's atan opt-in).
   The column biogeochemistry is refused by name.
   Also ported: the column part of the time step (the diagnostics' and
   fluxes' re-initialisations, `seaice_column_predynamics_time_integration`,
   `_dynamics_` and `_postdynamics_`, over several steps on the clock's day
   and time; `woof/hex/mpas_seaice/column_step_v842.py`,
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_column_step_parity.py`), and coupled into the
   forward time step with the velocity solver (`timestep_v842.advance`
   under `config_use_column_package`, the abort raised as `ColumnAbort`;
   `tests/test_mpas_seaice_coupled_step_parity.py`). Still to port: mushy
   vertical thermodynamics, the melt ponds' own physics, and the other `ice_atmo`
   options.
7. Biogeochemistry (`ice_zbgc*`, `ice_brine`, `ice_zsalinity`, `ice_algae`).
   Ported in phases (below); every flag a phase does not carry is refused by
   name. The NCAR aerosols (`config_use_aerosols`, `ice_aerosol`) are not
   biogeochemistry and stay refused until their own PR.

### Sea-ice biogeochemistry: the phases

In v8.4.2 every biogeochemistry flag needs
`config_use_column_biogeochemistry`, so `config_use_vertical_tracers` cannot
be turned on alone: it sits on the biogeochemistry framework and the brine
height. Every flag is off in the Registry, so each phase is opt-in. Each
phase lifts one refusal, adds an oracle stage or cases in
`tools/mpas_seaice_oracle/run_column.F90` with its own fixture under
`tests/data/oracles/mpas_seaice_bgc_<phase>`, and is word-exact on CUDA and
Metal with mutation tests and an oracle rebuild test. v8.4.2's quirks are
kept by default, with opt-in corrections (`column_v842.CORRECTIONS`) where
they matter. The biogeochemistry calls only `exp` (the MIT glibc
transcription) and `**` (`pow`, `__powidf2`), so no new LGPL opt-in is
expected.

| Phase | Configs | Work | Oracle cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | `config_use_column_biogeochemistry` with `config_use_brine` only | the tracer object's brine fraction; `colpkg_init_hbrine`, `colpkg_init_zbgc`'s brine part, `init_column_biogeochemistry_profiles`, the coupler's ocean concentrations and flux resets; `column_biogeochemistry`'s brine path (`preflushing_changes`, `compute_microS_mushy`, `update_hbrine`, `calculate_drho`) (`woof/hex/mpas_seaice/column_bgc_v842.py`, `tests/test_mpas_seaice_bgc_brine_parity.py`) | the init (BL99, mushy, a brine-height restart, three layers with CESM ponds); the stage with mushy and BL99, a short drainage length, four layers and three categories, and an abort |
| P1b | the same, through the rest of the column | the brine fraction carried through the ITD (`add_new_ice_bgc`, `zap_small_areas`), ridging, the column step (`column_biogeochemistry` after the vertical thermodynamics; `seaice_column_reinitialize_diagnostics_bgc`, refused without the correction `bgc_inactive_diagnostics` because v8.4.2 zeroes the unallocated `totalChlorophyll`) and the coupled step (`coupling_prep`'s and `scale_fluxes`' ocean biogeochemistry fluxes); the remap already carried it and its oracle cases test it (`tests/test_mpas_seaice_bgc_brine_carry_parity.py`) | five ITD and three ridging cases; the column step over 3 steps (three cases); the coupled step with ridging (two cases, without advection) |
| P2 | `config_use_vertical_zsalinity` (needs brine and BL99) | v8.4.2 never sets the column package's `solve_zsal` (`colpkg_init_parameters`) and copies `verticalSalinity` only with the vertical tracers, so by default the vertical salinity is inert: the tracer count, the init's Rayleigh criterion, the brine height's step, BL99's `dTemp` 0.02 (`tests/data/oracles/mpas_seaice_bgc_zsal`). The opt-in correction `zsal_solve` solves it: `compute_microS`, `update_hbrine`'s `dh_direct`, `ice_zsalinity.F90` (`zsalinity`, `solve_zsalinity`, `solve_S_dt`, the salt fluxes, `column_sum_zsal`, `merge_zsal_fluxes`) and `dTemp` 0.1, held to an oracle built with `tools/mpas_seaice_oracle/zsal_solve.patch` (`tests/test_mpas_seaice_bgc_zsal_parity.py`) | inert: the init, the stage, a remap abort, BL99's thermodynamics; `zsal_solve`: the init and its restart, the stage (the Rayleigh criterion met and not, a brine height above `maxhbr`, one substep, a molecular sublayer), the remap and `max_salin` aborts, BL99's thermodynamics in winter and in a melt season |
| P2b | the same, through the rest of the column | the zsal tracers carried by the ITD, ridging, the column step and the coupled step (inert by default); under `zsal_solve` the ITD's and `therm_itd`'s zsal branches | the ITD, ridging, the column step and the coupled step, inert and under `zsal_solve` |
| P3 | `config_use_vertical_tracers` with `config_use_nonreactive` only | the z-tracer core: the bio grid, mobile fractions, transport without reactions (the FCT matrix and correction, `tridiag_solverz`, `thin_ice_flux`, the conservation check), new ice and lateral melt, ridging, the remap, the snow and ice tracer split, the ocean fluxes | the stage with thin and thick ice; the column step with the ITD; the coupled step with advection |
| P4 | `config_use_zaerosols`, `config_use_shortwave_bioabsorption` (needs P3) | the aerosol z-tracers; chlorophyll and aerosols in the delta-Eddington shortwave (`dEdd_algae`) | z-aerosol transport; the radiation stage; the column step |
| P5a | `config_use_vertical_biochemistry` | `algal_dyn`, `zbio`: algae, nitrate, ammonium, silicate | the stage; the column step; a nutrient-limited case |
| P5b | the same | carbon (DOC, DIC), DON, DMS, iron, humics | the same per species group |
| P6 | `config_use_skeletal_biochemistry` | the bottom layer's biogeochemistry (`sklbio`, `skl_biogeochemistry`); exclusive with the z-tracers, so it can follow P1 directly | the stage; the column step |

The order is P1, P1b, P2, P2b, P3, P4, P5a, P5b; P6 any time after P1, and the
NCAR aerosols as a small PR whenever it fits.

## MPAS-Ocean (`core_ocean`, about 80,000 lines)

1. Tracer advection, standard and monotone (`mpas_ocn_tracer_advection_{std,mono}.F`).
2. The split-explicit time integrator's tendencies. Ported: `vel_coriolis`,
   `vel_vadv`, `thick_hadv`/`vadv`, `vel_pressure_grad` (all five
   `config_pressure_gradient_type` values, with the common-level Jacobian) and
   `vel_hmix` (del2, Leith, del4). The POM/SCRUM, diagonal and pseudo-flux
   Jacobians in `mpas_ocn_vel_pressure_grad.F` are private and no config
   reaches them in v8.4.2. The tensor hmix forms (`mpas_tensor_operations`)
   are refused by name. The forcing terms (bulk forcing, surface stress,
   explicit bottom drag, Rayleigh friction, thickness surface flux) and their
   composition into `ocn_tend_vel` and `ocn_tend_thick` are ported. Refused
   by name: land-ice fluxes (since ported, bar HollandJenkins), frazil (since ported), the RKIND 8 bulk wind stress (glibc's
   double `cos`/`sin` have no permissive equal), and the bottom drag or
   Rayleigh bottom friction on a mesh with a dry edge (v8.4.2 indexes level 0
   there; opt-in `woof_bottom_drag_dry_edges = 'skip'` runs them with no
   drag on a dry edge). Rayleigh bottom friction keeps v8.4.2's interior
   coefficient and gating by default; `woof_rayleigh_bottom_friction =
   'corrected'` uses the bottom coefficient whenever the bottom friction is
   on. The ISOMIP land-ice freezing point keeps v8.4.2's boundary-layer
   temperature as its salinity by default;
   `woof_land_ice_isomip_freezing_salinity = 'boundary_layer_salinity'`
   corrects it. Still to port (at the time): `ocn_tend_tracer`, then the barotropic subcycle
   (`mode_forward/mpas_ocn_time_integration_split.F`).
3. Equation of state (`equation_of_state_jm`, `_linear`) and diagnostics.
4. Vertical mixing: constant and Richardson (`vmix_coefs_*`). CVMix (KPP) is
   an external library fetched by `get_cvmix.sh`; it is ported or refused by
   name.
5. GM and Redi (`mpas_ocn_gm.F`, `tracer_hmix_redi`), forcing, and land-ice
   fluxes.
6. Ecosystem/BGC tracers (`get_BGC.sh` externals). Refused by name.

## MALI (`core_landice`, about 22,000 lines)

1. The SIA velocity solver (`mode_forward/mpas_li_sia.F`), the default
   `config_velocity_solver`. `li_sia_solve` is the first target. Its edge loop
   is short, but its extraction is wider than the sea-ice or ocean ones:
   - It calls `li_calculate_flowParamA` (`mpas_li_thermal.F`), which needs
     `exp`.
   - It calls the barycentric vertex interpolation
     (`mpas_cells_to_points_using_baryweights`, `framework/mpas_geometry_utils.F`)
     or the kite-area one (`li_setup`).
   - It reads the `li_mask` bit tests.
   - It raises to real powers (`x**(n-1)`, `x**(n+1)` with a real
     `config_flowLawExponent`), which gfortran compiles to `powf`/`pow`.

   So the oracle compiles `li_constants`, `li_setup`, `li_mask`, `li_thermal`
   and `mpas_geometry_utils` verbatim, and the stand-ins grow to
   `mpas_timekeeping`, `mpas_log`, `mpas_abort` and
   `mpas_vector_reconstruction`. The GPU side takes `powf` and `expf` from the
   hex lane's qualified glibc transcriptions (`woof.hex.native_powf`,
   `glibc_flt32`). At RKIND 8 the double `pow`/`exp` are `glibc_flt64`'s
   (Arm optimized-routines, MIT), matched to the oracle host's glibc. Every
   ported MALI piece is word-exact at RKIND 4 and RKIND 8; at RKIND 8 the
   floating melt 'seroussi' (`sin`) is refused by name; 'mismip' (`tanh`) runs
   on CORE-MATH's correctly rounded `tanh`, which glibc 2.44 carries. The
   RKIND 8 refusals that rest on glibc's binary64 `sin`, `cos` and `atan`
   (this one and those in 2 and 4) are lifted by an opt-in LGPL
   transcription of glibc's own (`WOOF_GLIBC239_LIBM64=1` in a source
   checkout; `tools/glibc239_libm_gpu`, NOTICE), off by default and in no wheel.
2. Thickness and tracer advection (`mpas_li_advection.F`) and the
   forward-Euler time integration. The diagnostics advection reads (masks,
   geometry, layer thickness, layer velocity and its CFL step) are ported,
   and so is `li_advection_thickness_tracers` ('fo' and 'none', with and
   without tracers): `tracer_setup`/`tracer_finish`, the upwind advection,
   `apply_mass_balance` and `vertical_remap`. The forward-Euler time step
   (`li_time_integrator_forwardeuler`) is ported whole for the SIA and 'none'
   velocity solvers: `prepare_advection` (the apparent diffusivity, the CFL
   limits and the fixed or adaptive step), the floating melt, the thermal
   solver, `advection_solver`, `li_velocity_solve` (the reconstruction, strain
   rates and fast-ice removal) and `li_calculate_diagnostic_vars`
   (`woof/hex/mpas_ice/time_integration_fe_v842.py`). Refused by name inside
   the step: at RKIND 8 the sphere's reconstruction
   (glibc's binary64 `cos`/`sin`) and the strain-rate angle `eTheta`
   (glibc's binary64 `atan`, LGPL and not correctly rounded), unless the
   opt-in glibc transcription is on.
3. The thermal solver (`mpas_li_thermal.F`): enthalpy/temperature column
   solves. Ported: the initialisation and the conversions, and
   `li_thermal_solver` (SIA heat dissipation, basal friction, the
   temperature and enthalpy matrix elements with `dsigmaTerm`,
   `tridiag_solver`, the conductive fluxes, grounded basal melt) with
   `li_basal_melt_floating_ice` ('none', 'file', 'constant', 'mismip',
   'seroussi'). The heat dissipation's zero row for an edge without two cells
   is ported but not reached by the oracle, whose masks need every edge's
   cells inside the mesh.
4. Calving, subglacial hydrology, masks and diagnostics. Calving is ported:
   `li_calve_ice` for every v8.4.2 option ('thickness_threshold', 'floating',
   'topographic_threshold', 'eigencalving' with a 'scalar' or 'data'
   parameter, the calving timescale and `config_data_calving`) and
   `li_restore_calving_front` (`woof/hex/mpas_ice/calving_v842.py`, held
   inside the forward-Euler step's fixture). Subglacial hydrology is ported:
   `li_SGH_solve` with its subcycling, the sheet and channels, 'cavity' and
   'overburden' pressure, every basal melt input and tangent-slope
   calculation, and the SHMIP C and D forcings (at RKIND 8 the SHMIP
   forcings are refused by name without the opt-in: glibc's binary64 `sin`/`cos`)
   (`woof/hex/mpas_ice/subglacial_hydro_v842.py`).
5. The forward mode around the step (`mpas_li_core.F`,
   `mpas_li_time_integration.F`; `woof/hex/mpas_ice/forward_v842.py`):
   `li_simulation_clock_init` (start, `config_dt`, run duration or stop time
   on 'gregorian_noleap' or 'gregorian', the adaptive step's force alarm),
   `li_core_init` (`li_setup_config_options`, the time stamps, `deltat` and
   `daysSinceStart`, the vertical grid, the edge signs, the barycentric
   weights, the initial mask, `li_thermal_init` and `li_SGH_init`),
   `li_core_initial_solve` and the `li_core_run` loop (`li_timestep`, the
   time-level shift, the force alarm's reset), with its own bitwise restart
   (`write_restart`/`read_restart`). Held word for word at RKIND 4 and 8 on
   CUDA and Metal against the land-ice core, `mpas_timekeeping` and the ESMF
   time library compiled verbatim, over the MALI test cases' physics (dome
   fixed and adaptive, dome with calving, EISMINT2 A with the temperature and
   enthalpy solvers, the radial hydrology test and a marine variant, the
   enthalpy benchmark), each on a uniform and a variable resolution periodic
   hexagonal mesh (`tests/test_mali_forward_parity.py`). Refused by name:
   MPAS's restart stream (`config_do_restart`), RK4, the statistics, the
   analysis members, intervals with months, years on the Gregorian calendar,
   fractions of a second. `li_SGH_init` gives grounded ice below sea level
   the ocean's water pressure (it reads a mask holding only the initial
   extent bit); `woof_sgh_init_mask = 'corrected'` computes the mask first,
   off by default. With the enthalpy solver, `tracer_setup`/`tracer_finish`
   convert the advected enthalpy with Kelvin temperatures against the
   thermal solver's Celsius melting point, so the ice turns temperate and
   melts; `woof_enthalpy_advection_units = 'corrected'` converts in Celsius,
   off by default. `woof hex ice run` (`woof/hex/mpas_ice/run_v842.py`)
   runs the forward mode from a MALI namelist (every option at its v8.4.2
   Registry default, `Registry_subglacial_hydro.xml` included) and an MPAS
   land-ice input file, and writes `output.nc` and a bitwise `restart.npz`;
   a case written out as such a file runs to the oracle's words
   (`tests/test_mali_run_door.py`). The input must carry
   `edgeNormalVectors` and `coeffs_reconstruct` (MPAS's reconstruction
   initialisation is not ported). MALI's side of the land-ice/ocean seam
   (`woof/hex/mpas_ice/ocean_seam_v842.py`, names agreed with the ocean
   coupling): exports `landIcePressure`, `landIceFraction`, `landIceMask`,
   `li2ocn_liquid_runoff` (the hydrology's discharge across the grounding
   line into ocean cells) and `li2ocn_solid_runoff` (the step's calving),
   with `landIceDraft` as a diagnostic; imports
   `ocn2li_floating_basal_mass_bal` through v8.4.2's
   `config_basal_mass_bal_float = 'file'`. Calving momentum is refused by
   name (v8.4.2 computes none). No oracle exists for the seam; it is graded
   by conservation (`tests/test_mali_ocean_seam.py`). The core is
   `ported_subset` for the 'sia', 'simple' and 'none' velocity solvers.
   'simple' (`woof/hex/mpas_ice/velocity_simple_v842.py`) is
   `li_velocity_simple_block_init`'s prescribed field ('uniform' or 'radial',
   with its circular-shelf bed for 'floating' and 'topographic_threshold'
   calving) copied into `normalVelocity` each step and zeroed off the
   dynamic ice; three forward cases on both meshes hold it to the oracle's
   words. The higher-order package's `dirichletVelocityMask` is carried
   unchanged: only the Albany solvers read it. The statistics are next.
6. **The Albany first-order (Blatter-Pattyn) Stokes solver**
   (`config_velocity_solver` = 'FO', also 'L1L2' and 'Stokes'). This is a C++
   finite-element code built on Trilinos (Kokkos assembly, NOX/Belos/MueLu
   nonlinear and linear solves), reached through
   `Interface_velocity_solver.cpp`. It is not part of MPAS-Model. A word-exact
   oracle needs a pinned Albany + Trilinos build. Even then, parallel
   multigrid and Krylov reductions are not bit-reproducible across
   decompositions, so the reference would be a single-rank,
   fixed-ordering build. Until that build exists, these solver values are
   refused by name. The FE pieces that can be pinned (element assembly of
   the FO viscosity and the basal friction residual) are the first
   candidates.

## The coupler (E3SM v3.2.0, `driver-mct`)

MPAS's ocean and sea ice exchange fluxes through E3SM's coupler, not through
anything in MPAS-Model. The coupler pieces are transcribed from E3SM tag
v3.2.0 (`648b167b`) and held to the routines compiled verbatim with gfortran
-O0 -ffp-contract=off on the MCT commit that tag pins (`82b0071`), in
`tools/e3sm_coupler_oracle/`. Each is opt-in: the coupled step keeps its
matched exchange unless asked.

1. The ocean import merge, `prep_ocn_merge` (`driver-mct/main/prep_ocn_mod.F90`).
   Ported (`woof/hex/coupled/prep_ocn_merge_e3sm.py`,
   `tests/test_e3sm_ocean_merge_parity.py`): the field matching and its
   aborts, the zero and shared-name copies, the shortwave, precipitation and
   runoff, the nutrients, the per-category fields and the generic
   fraction-weighted merge, real(r8), on CUDA and Metal. The water isotopes
   are refused by name. The sea ice's import merge, `prep_ice_merge`
   (`driver-mct/main/prep_ice_mod.F90`), is ported too
   (`woof/hex/coupled/prep_ice_merge_e3sm.py`,
   `tests/test_e3sm_ice_merge_parity.py`): the shared-name copies from o2x,
   a2x, g2x and (two-way waves) w2x into an x2i it does not zero, and rain,
   snow and the ice runoff summed, then scaled by `flux_epbalfact`.
2. The fractions on the ocean grid (`seq_frac_set`, `ifrac`/`ofrac` from the
   ice's `Si_ifrac`) and the radiative fractions (`ifrad`/`ofrad`, from
   `seq_flux_ocnalb_mct`). `seq_frac_set` is ported
   (`woof/hex/coupled/seq_frac_e3sm.py`, `tests/test_e3sm_seq_frac_parity.py`):
   the ice grid's fractions from `Si_ifrac` and the domain's `frac`, their
   maps to the ocean and atmosphere grids through `seq_map_map` (copy,
   rearrangement, or the sparse matrix multiply in MCT's element order) and
   `seq_frac_check`'s stops (or, with dead models, its reports), real(r8),
   on CUDA and Metal, against the routines compiled verbatim on the pinned
   MCT. Its eps is E3SM's single-precision `1.0e-02`. The radiative
   fractions are still to port.
3. The atmosphere/ocean fluxes, `xao` (`seq_flux_atmocn_mct`, `shr_flux_atmocn`).
   Ported (`woof/hex/coupled/flux_atmocn_e3sm.py`,
   `tests/test_e3sm_flux_atmocn_parity.py`): `seq_flux_init_mct`'s mask,
   `emask` (E3SM's single-precision `1.0e-12` density limit), the E3SMv1
   scheme (`ocn_surface_flux_scheme = 0`) with the explicit and implicit
   stress (`shr_flux_update_stress`), gustiness, the cold-air outbreak
   scaling, `flux_convergence`/`flux_max_iteration`, the minimum wind and
   dead models, and E3SM's and MCT's stops, real(r8), on CUDA and Metal,
   against the routines compiled verbatim on the pinned MCT. `psimhu` calls
   glibc's atan, so the port needs the opt-in glibc 2.39 transcription
   (`WOOF_GLIBC239_LIBM64=1`) and is refused by name without it; exp, log
   and pow are the MIT transcriptions. COARE (1), UA (2), the diurnal cycle,
   the two-way wave drag and the water isotopes are refused by name.
   The ocean albedos, `seq_flux_ocnalb_mct`, are ported too
   (`woof/hex/coupled/ocnalb_e3sm.py`, `tests/test_e3sm_ocnalb_parity.py`):
   `flux_albav`'s namelist albedos with the radiative fractions, and the net
   shortwave `Faox_swdn`/`Faox_swup` from xao's albedos (0 on the dark side),
   with E3SM's and MCT's stops, real(r8), on CUDA and Metal. The orbital
   albedo (`shr_orb_decl`/`shr_orb_cosz`, glibc's sin, cos and asin) is
   refused by name. The ocean-input accumulation, `prep_ocn_accum` and
   `prep_ocn_accum_avg` over MCT's `mct_avect_accum`/`mct_aVect_avg`, is
   ported (`woof/hex/coupled/ocn_accum_e3sm.py`,
   `tests/test_e3sm_ocn_accum_parity.py`): the window's sum, the average by
   `1/count` rounded once (E3SM's reciprocal, kept) and the copy back into
   x2o, real(r8), on CUDA and Metal. One coupler interval chains them
   (`woof/hex/coupled/interval_e3sm.py`,
   `tests/test_e3sm_coupler_interval_parity.py`): `cime_comp_mod`'s
   RASM_OPTION1 phases (`cime_run_atmocn_setup`, `_ocn_setup_send`,
   `_ice_setup_send`, `_update_fractions`) over several coupler steps, an
   ocean and sea ice on one grid (the copy_only mappers) and an atmosphere
   mapper of any kind, against E3SM's own routines cut verbatim and linked
   into one Fortran driver (`run_coupler_interval.F90`), word for word after
   every step on CUDA and Metal. The flux's mask is taken once from the
   starting fractions, as `seq_flux_init_mct` takes it (kept). The wave
   couplings are refused by name. The interval runs on the spine
   (`woof/hex/coupled/run_e3sm.py`, `tests/test_e3sm_coupled_spine.py`).
   It is driven over a `CoupledClock` with the alarms cut from the ocean's
   and sea ice's coupling periods. The components (`Components`) are called
   where RASM_OPTION1 runs them, and every hand-over is a matched exchange.
   It is word-exact against the chained oracle with the components
   scripted. The alarms are E3SM's: `run_e3sm.Alarms` follows
   `seq_timemgr_mod`'s alarm set-up with the coupling offsets and
   `cime_comp_mod`'s startup ocean lag (`skip_ocean_run`), checked against a
   transcription of both, and the oracle holds a startup case. Next: the ocean's forward step and the sea ice's step as the
   components, through their ported import/export.
4. The ocean's and the sea ice's import and export
   (`components/mpas-ocean/driver/ocn_comp_mct.F`,

The matched exchange's land-ice/ocean channels (`woof/hex/coupled/exchange.py`)
are the ones agreed with the MALI port's ocean seam. Land ice sends
`landIcePressure` (Pa), `landIceFraction`, `landIceMask` (int32), and
`li2ocn_liquid_runoff` and `li2ocn_solid_runoff` (kg m^-2 s^-1, positive into
the ocean). The ocean returns `ocn2li_floating_basal_mass_bal` in MALI's sign
(negative is melt). `landIceDraft` is a diagnostic channel only. The calving
momentum channels (`li2ocn_momentum_x`/`_y`; v8.4.2's land ice exports none)
and the old `li2ocn_mass` are refused by name.
